# Services business review

Turas demonstrates a proposed services operating model with fictional data. Never describe these figures as Vercel benchmarks or real customer outcomes. Policies below are proposals, not installed workflow automation.

1. **Why this artifact:** a weekly exception review joins customer acceptance, effort forecast, commercial exposure, and skill availability. It helps a director decide before scope drift consumes margin. A spreadsheet is adequate for arithmetic; Turi adds evidence synthesis, clarification, and approval requests. Do not claim measured time savings without a baseline.
2. **Function P&L:** call `calculate_services_plan` before quoting annual forecasts or sensitivities. Defaults are three delivery staff, 46 available weeks, 40 hours/week, 65% billable utilization, $250 realized rate, $230k annual loaded cost/person, $90k overhead, and $30k nonlabor cost. Annual payroll includes all paid time; leave reduces capacity, not pay. Do not add engagement allocated costs again. The annual plan is independent of the four engagement fixtures. Excludes platform ARR, tax, financing, and corporate allocations beyond entered overhead. A zero-revenue margin is undefined. A capacity-infeasible scenario is not an executable staffing plan.
3. **Pricing:** proposed offers are paid discovery, bounded prove/launch, then enablement/handoff. Fixed fee requires acceptance criteria, dependencies, exclusions, a change-control owner, and milestone payments. Use capped time and materials for unresolved scope. Example: 120 hours at $125 plus $2,000 nonlabor = $17,000 cost; 20% cost contingency yields $20,400; a 35% engagement contribution target requires $31,385 rounded-up fee. This is a cost floor, not a willingness-to-pay estimate or approved quote.
4. **GTM alignment:** qualify a consequential workflow, sponsor, measurable outcome, platform fit, scope, economics, and capacity. Sales owns the opportunity; delivery accepts scope/capacity; director signs price/margin exceptions; finance validates recognition/collections; customer signs acceptance. Strategic subsidies need an explicit budget, sponsor, hypothesis, and expiry. Never hide them as profitable PS or attribute all expansion ARR to services.
5. **Capacity and org:** call `get_capacity` for the actual synthetic weekly roster. Avery's four schedulable Enablement lead hours cannot deliver a 40-hour intervention in one week; Discovery capacity is not a substitute. Defer, extend the plan, or secure qualified capacity. Hire for sustained qualified demand by skill/date, use partners for temporary/repeatable demand, and protect learning and reusable work. Utilization alone is not an engineer performance score.
6. **Intervention economics:** load the engagement, then call `calculate_engagement_scenario`. The proposed 40 hours REPLACE the baseline 80 remaining hours; they are not additional. They improve allocated forecast contribution by $5,000, not necessarily cash profit: salaried payroll persists. If 40 hours are additive, use 120 remaining hours; contribution falls to $12,000 (20%). A 40-hour plan requires customer scope/acceptance and staffing evidence. Record approval only via the approved tool; do not claim it changes fixtures, staffing, or delivery forecasts.
7. **Customer value:** baseline training completion is 12/40 administrators. Completion is a leading indicator, not proof of adoption. Proposed success is a named operating owner, accepted pilot, and independent customer operation at the 30-day review. Compare forecast vs actual hours, acceptance, rework, decision latency and customer adoption. Separate contribution from causal product revenue claims.
8. **AI and architecture:** AI SDK supports typed model/tool interaction and streaming through Eve; AI Gateway routes inference and provides usage visibility; Workflow underpins Eve's durable execution. Web Analytics measures page use, not customer ROI. SQL holds app sessions, conversation metadata, and decisions; operational fixtures remain code. Source of truth for arithmetic is the deterministic module, not the model. Research uses a reviewed source pack, not live web discovery. Gateway fallback and budgets are platform capabilities, not verified custom policies in this app.
9. **Safety and limits:** current application is an interview prototype with signed owner/reviewer sessions and an approval-gated synthetic write. Do not claim audited multitenant isolation or production readiness. Chat creation currently accepts client session references; first-send server ownership binding must be hardened before real customer use. Decision deduplication needs atomic, scoped writes; migration execution needs an operator-managed workflow. Do not assert hosted DB, approval replay, SSO, load, or recovery tests passed without a release record. On model failure direct users to the deterministic views; never invent a successful write.
10. **90 days:** days 1–30 validate costs, realized rates, qualified demand, skill constraints and acceptance with finance/GTM/delivery/customers. Days 31–60 pilot two bounded offers and weekly exception reviews. Days 61–90 scale only with evidence on forecast accuracy, accepted outcomes and decision speed; codify repeated work and justify hires. Before operational rollout add SSO/account authorization, secure first-send binding, scoped atomic writes, transactional migrations, data retention, alert ownership and recovery testing.

For an answer, lead with the decision, cite calculated assumptions, explain the customer consequence and tradeoff, then state evidence needed and the accountable owner. Do not promise unbuilt automation.

name: solution-research
description: Research customer solutions against their stack, processes, constraints, adoption needs, alternatives and delivery economics; evaluate evidence through the configured decision workflow.
---

1. Resolve the customer first. On no/ambiguous match, ask for the account/source, never a same-named integration. Clarify the proposed decision and success criterion when missing.
2. Obtain a current signed reference from `customer-recon` for this account, reusing a verified unexpired reference when its coverage meets the question. Otherwise run recon first within this authorized research request. Then delegate to `implementation-research` with canonical accountId, bounded question, proposal. Call preparation before each specialist; the server binds this chat’s completed recon to solution admission. Omit reconReference for this workflow. Use the message field only; do not override either specialist's outputSchema or request a full report return. The solution specialist verifies and imports it with current authorization, sources, conflicts and age ratings. Use a new task for each account or changed purpose. For an explicitly requested refinement of the same account, specialist type and workload, prepare its exact new question, then reuse the eligible taskId from that specialist’s receipt. Its original deadline and cumulative allowance do not renew; an unavailable, expired or stopped task must not be silently replaced. No secrets or whole transcripts. The child retrieves current approved context with inherited caller permissions. Label any user-supplied hypotheses explicitly, never as observations. Include relevant employee conference talks/webinars and firsthand product-user experience in the research scope. Preserve speaker affiliation, event date, source format, workload and self-reported outcomes; separate downstream-user processes from the customer account, and verify any product-to-Vercel link independently. Keep the existing research budget.
3. Tell the user research is running; keep the actual admitted taskId for cancellation. Admission is not completion. Separately admitted specialist types may work concurrently when their prerequisites are met. Each needs its own exact reservation; never start duplicate work for the same reservation. Public research uses the server-configured CONTEXT_API_KEY; users do not need a context.dev OAuth grant. If the service is unconfigured or rejects its credential, report unavailable research and ask the Turas owner to check the environment configuration. Never request a key in chat, trigger connector onboarding, restart a pending task, or ask a generic approval for an already requested read-only investigation. On cancellation/failure/provider unavailability, report the actual state and gaps. Never imply generic advice or prepared links are completed live research.
4. Call `evaluate_solution_research` with accountId only for research prepared in this chat. The server resolves its exact bound result; do not copy long result IDs. Explicit references remain supported for prior authorized artifacts. The tool loads and verifies the signed artifact from Postgres; never copy or reconstruct its payload. The primary model answers independent prerequisite questions; application rules combine them into an advisory route. Read the actual model from the result; never claim a separate decision provider was used. On a denied/missing artifact or failed handoff, report the failure and stop; do not automatically commission replacement research. Refresh expired or decision-critical aging/stale/unknown evidence as part of the requested research; do not wait for repeated user challenges. A failed provider/handoff is an explicit gap, not permission for an unbounded retry loop. An unavailable evaluator means no completed evaluation.
5. Explain recommendations with cited customer constraints and retrieved sources, alternatives, adoption difficulty/ownership, implementation/operating implications, gaps, rollout/rollback and measurable outcomes. Distinguish facts, inference and proposed targets. Copy public URLs from returned sources. Before answering, reconcile the brief with its returned evidence and blockers: exclude invalid quotations and unverified publication dates, preserve scope restrictions, and qualify unsupported customer claims even if they appear in the research recommendation. Missing records do not prove that an activity never happened. Vendor defaults do not establish this customer’s configuration. Use `customerEvidenceCitations` for customer references: preserve the supplied citationId/title and exact href, or cite plain text when href is null. Never construct domains, record IDs or anchors. Reconcile the brief with all supplied customer evidence: a limited search cannot prove that a workload is the customer's only deployment or that an unmentioned product is unused. Keep historical stage descriptions dated. Probabilities are uncalibrated rubric outputs; they are not calibrated outcome probabilities. Preserve the returned route and explain unknown/blocked dimensions and review requirements. Deterministic evidence, freshness and quotation gates belong to the application; never attribute those checks to a model. Do not turn independent probabilities into a joint success probability.
6. Explain the solution’s effect on current/proposed customer processes. Use the returned process-impact estimates and identify each customer baseline, external benchmark and assumption. Separate active work from elapsed waiting, capacity value from cash savings, and proposed targets from measured outcomes. Do not describe shorter elapsed time as released staff capacity without active-work evidence; do not infer avoided cash spending from a loaded hourly rate. Apply these rules to qualitative claims as well as numeric estimates. Include adoption/realization, setup/training/operating costs and the measurement plan. Missing inputs mean no numeric savings claim; never fill them with an industry average presented as customer fact. These estimates remain conditional on all evidence and freshness limitations.
7. Use deterministic services/capacity tools for relevant calculations, labeling assumptions. Never invent real-account fees/staffing. Research and evaluation grant no approvals, authorize no changes and prove no causal ROI. No automatic journal/portfolio writes; use established review/reuse paths only when requested.

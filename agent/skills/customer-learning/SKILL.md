name: customer-learning
description: Compare reviewed adoption outcomes across customers, or explicitly approve/revoke a source measurement for restricted portfolio learning, with comparable definitions and source lineage.
---
Use get_adoption_patterns for cross-customer adoption questions. Its report is a deterministic, restricted internal comparison over the caller's owned, separately contributed accounts. Request a completed calendar quarter. Do not search or concatenate account notes to manufacture a portfolio pattern. Reviewer access to a shared account does not grant portfolio access.

Explain the metric and equal-account weighting. Both intervention groups need at least five independent accounts; suppression is an evidence gap, not zero adoption. Do not estimate the suppressed count, identify contributors, report individual measurements, or derive hidden cells by repeated queries. No recorded intervention is not a verified untreated control. Association is not causation, company-wide maturity, attributable expansion or realized services ROI. Selection, missingness and baseline differences remain. Treat findings as hypotheses for a prospective delivery experiment.

Reuse requires separate human approval through review_learning_contribution. First resolve the canonical account, retrieve its current approved journey source and inspect get_learning_contribution for the quarter/version. Confirm that the source measures Weekly active administrators in percent units, is measured (not estimated/reported), compares seven-day active windows 28–35 days apart, uses the same eligible administrator population and one governed execution pilot, and defines active as at least one governed execution. Get baseline/current counts, the eligible count and intervention category from the supplied evidence; never invent them. Counts must match the approved percentages. Quarter is determined by the end measurement date. Explain exactly what will be reused and that raw notes, identities and commercial terms stay private; request the tool's explicit owner approval.

One account contributes at most once per metric/quarter. A replacement requires the latest version and fresh approval. A correction/retraction makes the previous source ineligible immediately. Use revoke with the latest contribution version to withdraw reuse independently of the account note; keep the same requestId only for an exact retry. Do not claim inclusion means the minimum cohort has been met. Link back to the account journey for source review. On unavailable, denied, conflict or unsupported metric, explain the gap and recovery action; never claim a successful write or report.

## Implementation strategy and completion criteria

US1 is the MVP demonstration: create/read a supported or discovery-only proposal.
It is not completion of 011. Add owner decisions, then ranking/lifecycle, then advice;
run each independent scenario before broadening. Keep task checkboxes unchecked until
actual evidence exists. Full feature completion requires all four stories, all gates
and truthful documentation. A blocked external/live/hosted gate is reported explicitly
and cannot be replaced by a mock, a skipped test or a claim that merged equals hosted.

# 012 Data Model

Contract versions: `product-gaps-v1`, `gap-impact-v1`, `gap-order-v1`, `gap-detail-v1`, `gap-portfolio-v1`. This is design, not an installed schema. Migrations 048 and 049 are provisional after current 047; implementation must recheck the manifest before creating new migrations and must never edit applied 041 or other historical migrations.

## Canonical constraints

These sentences are normative. Tasks quote each constraint at its implementation point.

- **C01**: All persisted identities are UUIDs scoped by environment and workspace; versions and generations are positive integers; each mutation requires a UUID requestKey, expectedVersion and contractVersion product-gaps-v1.
- **C02**: Gap kind is product_gap, defect, documentation or enablement; title is 1–200 characters, productKey is a current canonical product key or unknown, capability is 1–200 characters, and productVersion is null or 1–100 characters.
- **C03**: Problem, workflow, desiredOutcome, reproduction, constraints, workaround, workaroundLimitations, proposedCapability, alternatives, unknowns and requestedDecision are each 1–4000 characters; triageTeam is 1–200 characters; acceptanceCriteria has 1–20 strings of 1–1000 characters; severity is critical, high, medium, low or unknown with a 1–2000-character rationale; workaroundFeasibility is none, limited, feasible or unknown.
- **C04**: An impact observation names one canonical customer, optional same-customer workload and engagement, a non-future observedAt timestamp or null, 1–4000-character consequences and workaroundBurden, severity critical, high, medium, low or unknown with a 1–2000-character rationale, and up to 20 assertions; each assertion has 1–2000-character text, classification accepted_fact, attributed_research, estimate or unknown, and 0–20 local source keys.
- **C05**: Each revision has at most 50 selected original sources and 200 transitive original dependencies; a source binds kind, original revision UUID, positive generation, SHA256 content digest and exact original locator; supported kinds are accepted_profile, approved_excerpt, verified_research, shared_knowledge, execution_record and milestone_baseline.
- **C06**: Impact classification is suspected, confirmed or resolved; confirmed requires a known non-future observedAt, accepted customer need/impact and current direct product evidence; resolved requires a known non-future observedAt and known non-future resolution time at or after observedAt plus accepted customer-resolution evidence; recurrence creates a new observation linked to the prior resolved observation.
- **C07**: Gap disposition is open, deferred or dismissed; every review/reclassification/retraction has a 1–2000-character rationale; defer requires a future UTC revisitDate within 366 days; only canonical active internal administrator mcteer may decide; self-authored approval requires an explicit selfReview acknowledgment.
- **C08**: Decision and canonicalization previews belong to one actor/session, expire after five minutes and bind exact revisions, generations, original-source closure and requested action; an exact successful replay may return a minimal authorized receipt after preview expiry but may never repeat the mutation.
- **C09**: One merge selects 2–20 active canonical gaps of the same kind and productKey and one survivor; one split selects one canonical gap and 2–10 results including the original identity and fresh additional gaps; either operation covers at most 1000 observations and requires every existing observation to have exactly one explicit resulting canonical assignment.
- **C10**: List pages contain 1–50 rows; search text is 0–200 characters; cursors bind actor, workspace, filter and ordering generation; count projections cover at most 2000 gaps, 10000 observations and 20000 dependency headers, failing explicitly rather than returning partial totals.
- **C11**: A report is detail or portfolio, selects 1 gap for detail or 0–100 explicit canonical gaps for portfolio, contains at most 200 customers and 2000 original dependencies, and has an asOf timestamp plus an optional earlier comparisonAt no more than 90 days before asOf; future timestamps are rejected.
- **C12**: A report audience has a 1–200-character team, 1–25 named recipients of 1–200 characters each and a 1–2000-character purpose; every included canonical customer requires an explicit disclosure acknowledgment bound to that report revision and audience digest.
- **C13**: Each report revision is at most 2 MiB of canonical structured content; exports are UTF-8 Markdown and JSON, each at most 5 MiB; private files use opaque UUID keys, SHA256 digests and exact byte lengths; report preparation has a 30-second deadline, one active job per actor and five per workspace, and no automatic preparation retry.
- **C14**: Handoff kind is handed_off, acknowledged, investigating, resolution_reported or correction; eventAt is non-future, a first handoff is no earlier than report approval, recipient/team must match the approved audience, full-payload note is 1–4000 characters and reference is null or an HTTPS URL of at most 2000 characters with no embedded credentials; after report expiry/withdrawal only metadata-only follow-up/correction with no note, reference or evidence payload is permitted.
- **C15**: Original request receipts retain minimal authorized results for 365 days; thereafter an environment-lifetime keyed tombstone prevents key reuse; previews expire in five minutes; obsolete revision payloads expire after 90 days; report content and exports expire after 30 days; globally invalidated payloads and artifacts are purged within 24 hours while access is withheld immediately.
- **C16**: Per actor, admission allows 120 reads, 30 mutations/previews and five report preparations per rolling minute; mutation bodies are at most 256 KiB and report selection bodies at most 64 KiB; cleanup leases last 180 seconds and claim at most 100 records per transaction.

## Entities and relations

All tables carry environment/workspace and composite foreign keys where applicable. Runtime roles cannot update immutable decision, revision, assignment-event or receipt records. Content payload tables can be deleted only through narrowly granted maintenance procedures; metadata cannot retain prose after payload retirement.

### Gap identity, revision and decision

`product_gaps`: UUID, workspace/environment, monotonically versioned working/reviewed revision pointers, relation generation, canonical state (`active` or `redirected`), canonical target, creation time. Customer-independent canonical narrative must describe the general product problem, never customer identifiers or verbatim private customer passages. Customer evidence belongs to scoped impact observations. The UI may show authorized impact alongside the gap, but cannot save those disclosures in its common narrative.

`gap_revisions` and `gap_revision_payloads`: parent identity, revision number, canonical content digest, creator/time, C02–C03 content, selected product/shared-research source references. Product evidence is a selected eligible original; mere absence from documentation is not proof of unsupported behavior. A revision can retain unknown product version and discovery questions, but confirmed impact still requires evidence directly establishing the relevant limitation at the applicable version/as-of.

`gap_decisions`: reviewed revision, action (`accept`, `reject`, `defer`, `dismiss`, `reopen`), old/new disposition, exact binding digest, rationale, reviewer identity, self-review acknowledgment, revisit date and time. Rejecting proposed edits does not replace the last reviewed head. Defer/dismiss/reopen may use a metadata-only preview after source withdrawal; this changes disposition only and cannot approve/release invalidated content. Reclassifying a gap requires a new revision and ordinary review.

`gap_revision_states`: mutable generation, current eligibility/withholding state and payload expiry. Current working/reviewed payloads remain while eligible; obsolete payloads expire under C15. A dismissed gap remains readable as permitted history, but is absent from default active ordering. Product availability is described as evidence-backed authored content; it is not an automatic gap/impact lifecycle transition.

### Customer impact and original dependencies

`gap_impact_observations`: immutable original gap identity, canonical customer, optional workload/engagement, recurrence parent, working/reviewed revision pointers, version and active/retired state. Multiple observations are allowed; they are never treated as distinct customers. A retired observation contributes no current count. Changing customer identity requires a new observation plus reviewed retirement of the mistaken observation; IDs are not overwritten.

`gap_impact_revisions`/payloads/states and `gap_impact_decisions`: C04–C06 content and exact review; `accept`, `reject`, `retire` decisions with C07 authority. Resolved observations are terminal; correction uses a new reviewed revision without silently deleting earlier history, while a new recurrence uses a new observation. Confirmed → suspected is an explicit reviewed downgrade with rationale; source invalidation independently withholds current eligibility and never rewrites the old decision as suspected.

`gap_source_dependencies`: restricted original-source kind/revision/generation/digest/locator and customer binding, assertion purpose and classification, reviewed quality inputs plus original timestamps, current-state reference and affected payload/report IDs. Original-source closure is stored; plans, reports, gap summaries and raw artifact uploads are not substitute citations. Execution and milestone links expand to their original eligible dependencies and bind current accepted heads, scope and non-stale baseline. No chat/conversation, workforce/finance, support or expansion payload is a new evidence source in 012.

Confirmation requires each critical original to remain authorized, conflict-free, current and score at least 60 under evidence-quality-v1. Customer-impact assertions require accepted internal/profile or approved excerpt evidence, directness at least 3 and freshness Recent/Aging; product limitation assertions require directness 4 and Recent product evidence with the policy's relevant 14/30-day window and unexpired assessment. Execution records can corroborate reviewed impact but cannot replace accepted customer-need evidence. Weak, insufficient, undated critical, stale or contradictory evidence stays discovery-only. Shared knowledge proves its published pattern, not a named customer's experience. Rechecks use the original header, current generation and dates; no model or new source acceptance path is added.

### Canonical merge/split graph

`gap_canonicalization_decisions` and `gap_assignment_events`: append-only operation, exact input/output gaps, source relation generations, one assignment for every affected observation, reviewer/time/rationale and new generations. `gap_observation_assignments` is the current materialized assignment derived from events; unique observation identity guarantees one current canonical gap. Never rewrite an observation's origin/customer or its old review. Merge unions observations into the survivor. Redirects are flattened in the same transaction to one active survivor; forbid self/cyclic targets and cross-workspace/kind/product merges. Survivor content is an explicitly selected reviewed revision; combining content requires a separately reviewed revision first.

Split retains the original identity for one explicitly selected result and creates fresh additional gaps. Its preview binds all resulting content and source closures; the mcteer decision atomically accepts those exact revisions and the complete observation partition, retaining prior reviewed content as history. Every observation, including retired/history rows, is assigned exactly once. If authors need evidence for two distinct needs, create and review a separate linked observation after the split. This permits genuinely distinct customer needs without automatic count inflation. Over-C09 operations fail; smaller valid operations may be explicitly reviewed, with no hidden multi-transaction partial merge.

## Deterministic impact and ordering

A count requires both an eligible reviewed canonical gap and eligible reviewed impact in that gap’s current product/kind context. At a cutoff, select the latest reviewed impact decision at/before cutoff, map its observation through the current canonical assignment graph, and apply current actor/customer/source eligibility and source quality evaluated at the cutoff AND now. This is a recomputed historical view, never a claim that it reproduces a past user's visibility. Missing/purged required history makes that comparison unavailable, not zero.

For each canonical gap and customer: at least one eligible confirmed current observation places it in `currentConfirmed`; otherwise at least one eligible suspected current observation places it in `currentSuspectedOnly`. `resolvedHistorical` counts distinct customers with an eligible reviewed resolved observation at/before cutoff. It may overlap current categories after recurrence; the UI/report explicitly says these categories are not additive. Retired observations do not contribute current impact, and superseded versions are never counted as separate observations. Merged aliases do not create gap rows. Non-product-gap kinds have separately labeled lists and are excluded from product-gap portfolio totals.

Counts are computed before disposition filtering: deferral/dismissal does not erase factual customer impact. Reports state their selected dispositions. Current source header checks are batched and metadata-only for counts. They must match source-reader eligibility in parity tests; a cache/job flag alone cannot authorize a count. Source payloads are fetched only for authorized detail/report release. Above C10 bounds, return `scope_too_large`, never a count based on the first page. No hidden global totals or omitted-customer counts are returned. Comparisons recompute both cutoffs using the same current authorization, canonical assignments, method and comparable source set; explain source/canonical changes and withhold trend when comparability fails.

Ordering `gap-order-v1`: due revisit first; then current reviewed severity (`critical`, `high`, `medium`, `low`, `unknown`), confirmed count descending, workaround (`none`, `limited`, `unknown`, `feasible`), oldest last-review timestamp, canonical UUID. Display evidence freshness/withheld flags alongside the tuple; freshness is a review signal, not an invented numeric severity multiplier. Proposed working severity never changes reviewed ordering. Only default active (`open`, `deferred`) product gaps participate.

## Reports, jobs, exports and disclosure

`gap_reports`/`gap_report_revisions`/payloads/states`: stable report identity; exact C11 selection, template/method versions, current reviewed gap/impact heads, original closure, canonical relation generations, counts and section document, content digest, asOf/comparisonAt, creator and expiry. An unknown or inaccessible selected ID rejects the whole request; no silent subset. Zero-result portfolio is explicit and valid. asOf/comparisonAt govern impact events and evidence evaluation; the document labels canonical narrative and relations as the current reviewed view, not as a historical snapshot. A gap with no reviewed impact at the cutoff has zero eligible impact, never future events backdated into its count.

`gap_report_jobs`: actor/session-bound preparation identity and request digest; `queued → running → prepared | failed | cancelled`, one admitted run, lease/deadline/cancel timestamps and safe failure category. The absolute deadline is admission time plus 30 seconds; the claim lease cannot outlive it. Queue time is included. A crashed/lost running preparation is failed on lease/deadline expiry; it is not automatically regenerated. An explicit new request after reconciliation can prepare a new revision. Pure deterministic work has no provider calls. Release rechecks current actor, every selected scope/head/source and the original binding before storing the result. Private scratch objects have exact manifests for cleanup after interruption.

`gap_report_reviews` and `gap_report_disclosures`: mcteer-only exact report/audience/selection/source binding, per-customer acknowledgment, rationale/self-review, approval time and validity. Editing audience or report produces a new version and requires a new review. Ordinary internal users can read currently authorized draft reports; only current mcteer can approve, download or record handoff. Approval does not broaden ordinary source access. A downloaded artifact includes a scope/classification marker and approved audience; public/partner publication remains forbidden.

`gap_export_artifacts` and `gap_export_receipts`: Markdown/JSON formats, exact object key/digest/size, generation state and report review identity. Never reuse 009's automatic review-ready download eligibility. Export bytes include no hidden source metadata, secret URLs or private storage keys. JSON is a reviewed recipient document/manifest (stable visible citation labels/digests and approved scope), not a dump of dependency tables. Regeneration under the same exact approved revision is deterministic and cannot extend retention. Lost export acknowledgments reconcile to the same artifact identity; stream revocation prevents later chunks.

## Handoff and audit lifecycle

`gap_handoff_receipts`: report/review/artifact digests, approved audience digest, C14 event payload, reporter, request identity and predecessor/correction link. Initial handoff requires a current eligible approved unexpired report; later follow-up/correction may append permitted metadata after expiry without releasing report prose or claiming a new send. Any attached response evidence must pass the existing original-source policy and remain attributed. Event payloads expire after 90 days once obsolete or 24 hours after global invalidation; minimum report/event identity and state survive in metadata.

Receipts, decisions and relation identities retain minimal audit for at least 365 days. After that, retain only referential identity, monotonic versions, disposition and required deduplication/redirect links while the associated records exist; remove actor/time/rationale payload where not needed. Request-key tombstones survive for the environment lifetime using the versioned keyring. Per-actor permission loss denies that actor immediately but does not delete otherwise valid workspace content. Global source withdrawal/customer retirement triggers payload withholding and C15 purge. Maintenance operates when feature creation is disabled and never derives permission from a stale receipt.

### Physical revision storage

Migration 048 stores gap and observation revisions/decisions in scoped unified
append-only tables with nullable `impact_id`; `gap_impact_revisions` and
`gap_impact_decisions` are filtered views. Composite revision/observation keys and
head constraints preserve the distinct logical identities above. Deletable prose
and immutable identity metadata remain separate. Gap acceptance decisions also
retain the explicit reviewer customer-independence acknowledgment.

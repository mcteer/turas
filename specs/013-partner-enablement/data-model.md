# Data Model: Partner Delivery and Enablement

Contract and deterministic progress version: `partner-enablement-v1`. New records are customer scoped except request deduplication and internal authority fences. No copying of accepted plan/execution state or shared-publication payloads.

## Normative constraints

These complete sentences are quoted in the implementation tasks. All lengths are Unicode characters after trimming; byte limits use UTF-8 serialized JSON. Unlisted properties are rejected.

- **C01**: Record, workspace, customer, engagement, plan and request identities are UUIDs; environmentId is the existing nonempty environment string; scope is required and immutable; workloadId is null or the engagement's workload; new aggregate revisions and organization authority generations are positive integers, existing membership revisions and expectedVersion are nonnegative integers, and createdAt/decisionAt are server UTC timestamps.
- **C02**: A guide title is 1–200 characters; a guide revision contains 1–20 lessons, 1–40 checkpoints and at most 128 KiB of content; lesson IDs and checkpoint IDs are unique UUIDs within the revision, order is contiguous from 1, and every checkpoint belongs to exactly one lesson.
- **C03**: Each lesson requires objective, what, how, why, alternatives, limitations, validation and escalation text of 1–2000 characters each; prerequisites and unknowns are arrays of 0–10 entries of 1–500 characters; each checkpoint has a 1–200-character title, 1–2000-character demonstration criterion, required boolean and 0–10 prerequisite checkpoint IDs forming an acyclic graph in the same revision; at least one checkpoint is required and a required checkpoint cannot depend on an optional checkpoint.
- **C04**: A guide selects 1–20 sources and a submission selects 0–20 sources, with at most 200 deduplicated original dependencies per operation; source kinds are accepted_profile, approved_excerpt, verified_research, shared_knowledge or accepted_execution, and accepted_execution is submission-only; every source binds exact revision, generation, digest, locator, original dates, classification and evidence-quality-v1 values without resetting evidence age.
- **C05**: Publish/verify requires every selected source to be current, authorized, delivery-visible, conflict-free and unexpired with quality at least 60 and directness at least 3; decision-critical product evidence must be Recent, other critical evidence Recent or Aging; customer facts require accepted_profile or approved_excerpt, and a verified demonstration requires at least one accepted_profile, approved_excerpt or accepted_execution source supporting the actual demonstration rather than general research.
- **C06**: Guide revision review state is draft, submitted, published or rejected; guide disposition is active or retired; publish/reject/retire and checkpoint verify/request_changes decisions require canonical active internal administrator mcteer, a 1–2000-character rationale and explicit selfReview=true when reviewing one's own authored content; retirement is terminal for that guide identity.
- **C07**: Review previews belong to one actor/session, bind exact action, target versions, audience, source closure and content digest, and expire after five minutes; a successful replay may return its minimal authorized receipt after expiry without repeating the mutation.
- **C08**: List page size defaults to 20 and is limited to 1–50; search is 0–200 characters; opaque cursors expire after 15 minutes and bind actor, environment, scope, filter and authority generation, with at most 100 live cursor records per actor and oldest-first eviction; list results expose hasMore/nextCursor but no unbounded global total.
- **C09**: A checkpoint attempt has 1–4000-character attempted and result text, 0–10 blockers of 1–500 characters, a required non-future observedAt UTC timestamp and draft or submitted state; a checkpoint has at most one undecided submitted attempt; corrections append immutable revisions and verification is terminal for that checkpoint within the assignment.
- **C10**: Each assignment binds one partner membership, its revision, organization ID and authority revision, customer grant revision, exact published guide revision and accepted plan baseline; stored state is active, withdrawn or superseded; at most one active assignment exists per member/guide and at most 100 per member/customer; authority or guide changes make it unavailable or obsolete until explicit replacement, with no inherited verification.
- **C11**: Only the assigned partner may author their checkpoint attempts; authorized internal members may read them and only mcteer may decide; another partner may never read attempt prose; assignment/withdrawal/replacement rationale is 1–2000 characters and replacement atomically supersedes the predecessor and creates a new identity.
- **C12**: Progress is verified required checkpoints divided by all required checkpoints on the exact current eligible assignment, with integer counts and percentage rounded down; optional checkpoints do not affect the fraction, and unavailable, obsolete or withdrawn assignments have null current progress rather than zero or inherited completion.
- **C13**: Every mutation has a UUID requestId, exact expectedVersion and immutable action/input digest scoped to environment and actor; identical authorized retries return one minimal receipt, changed input conflicts, unknown outcomes reconcile before any new action, and retired request identities can never be reused.
- **C14**: Each actor is limited to 120 reads and 30 mutations per minute and each workspace to 1200 reads and 300 mutations per minute; review previews count as mutations, commands have a ten-second deadline including a two-second lock timeout, and batches are not accepted.
- **C15**: Current eligible payloads remain; obsolete/withdrawn payloads expire after 90 days, globally ineligible payloads are withheld immediately and purged within 24 hours, previews expire in five minutes, and decision/receipt metadata is minimized after 365 days while environment-lifetime request tombstones prevent reuse; retries never extend the original deletion deadline.
- **C16**: Maintenance runs at least every 60 seconds in batches of at most 100 with a ten-second tick deadline; failures retry after 60 seconds and then up to five minutes with the original purge deadline unchanged; TURAS_013_DISABLED=1 blocks new work but permits authorized reads, guide retirement, assignment withdrawal, reconciliation and retention.

## Storage and relationships

Migration `050-partner-guides.cjs` introduces the guide and common command tables; `051-partner-learning.cjs` introduces learning. Both update `migrations/manifest.json` and schema version explicitly. Composite foreign keys enforce environment/workspace/customer ownership where those keys exist; domain transactions validate all cross-domain baseline/source joins. No request-time DDL or automatic migration.

### Guide aggregate

`partner_guides`: C01 stable identity, author, engagement/plan/workload binding, C06 disposition, monotonic version, nullable working/published revision pointers. `partner_guide_revisions`: immutable identity/number, author/time, exact accepted revision/baseline, content digest and contract version. `partner_guide_payloads`: C02/C03 title, lessons/checkpoints and C04 public reference descriptors. `partner_guide_revision_states`: C06 review state, eligibility generation and original invalidation/obsolete/deletion deadlines. Publication changes heads/state through an immutable decision; it does not rewrite the revision payload.

`partner_source_dependencies`: exact revision dependency headers plus separately restricted private shared lineage. All selected sources and the accepted plan are critical, so any invalid dependency withholds the whole body. No snippets or customer names in dependency/audit rows. Shared references project only the 005 public citation DTO.

`partner_review_decisions` and separately deletable `partner_decision_payloads`: guide/submission target, exact reviewed versions, reviewer/time, action, evidence digest, rationale and self-review acknowledgment. Body/rationale are subject to C15. Authorized minimal history exposes decision type/time and eligible actor attribution, never withdrawn content.

### Assignment and checkpoint aggregate

`partner_learning_assignments`: C10 binding, creator/time, C11 rationale payload, predecessor/successor links, stored lifecycle state and monotonic version. Current availability is computed separately from stored state: eligible, obsolete, authority_changed, source_unavailable or purged. Any current-guide revision mismatch is obsolete even if the old guide payload still exists.

`partner_checkpoint_attempts`: assignment/checkpoint identity and immutable attempt number. `partner_checkpoint_revisions`/`partner_checkpoint_payloads`: author/time, C09 observation/body/blockers, selected sources and C04/C05 evidence. Append a revision to a draft; submission freezes the exact head. A changes-requested decision closes that attempt and permits a new attempt; verified closes the checkpoint. A draft may describe a blocker with zero sources, but cannot be verified without accepted support. Verification requires all prerequisite checkpoints currently verified under the same assignment and current source checks of their evidence.

Progress is computed from the bounded current assignment/checkpoint/decision set using C12. If one previously verified prerequisite loses source eligibility, it no longer counts; affected dependents are unavailable pending a replacement assignment, not silently left verified. This does not alter original decisions or any delivery/skills record.

### Authority persistence

Reuse `memberships.revision` and `customer_grants.revision`. Migration 050 adds `partner_organizations.authority_revision` (positive, initially 1) and a monotonic trigger on active-state changes. A membership trigger ensures its existing revision increases on active/kind/principal/workspace/organization changes even when a maintenance update bypasses the normal service; preserve explicit larger service revisions. This prevents inactive→active or organization change→change-back from resurrecting old assignments. No new grant capability is introduced. Principal/session revocation still denies current access through existing live policy.

### Requests, previews and retention

`partner_commands`: C13 actor/environment/request uniqueness, action/input digest, result identity/version and terminal outcome; its transaction commits the domain mutation and receipt atomically. No separate durable executing job is needed. A lost response is unknown to the client until reconciled; a nonexistent request may be retried using the same identity and identical input. After C15 minimization keep request identity/action/digest and retired outcome only; never accept it as a new command.

`partner_list_cursors`: C08 server-side records addressed by random opaque UUIDs, binding actor/session, scope/authority revisions, query-filter digest, immutable sort key and expiry; no search prose or customer name. Reads resolve and reauthorize this binding. Expired/evicted cursors require a fresh page; reuse never extends expiry. Migration 050 includes this bounded discovery cache; maintenance deletes expired entries. No cursor signing key or operator keyring is added.

`partner_review_previews`: C07 private binding and expiry with bounded source closure; deletes payload at expiry. `partner_cleanup_jobs`: revision identity, reason, original event/deletion deadline, attempts and next attempt time. Use idempotent earliest-deadline updates. An eligible old revision becomes obsolete when superseded; source invalidation uses the earliest authoritative source invalidation/review-expiry timestamp, not the later worker discovery time. Customer-wide retirement is global invalidation; individual grant loss is a read fence, not deletion of otherwise eligible team guides. Assignment withdrawal/supersession starts its private payload's 90-day deadline. Decision/receipt metadata is retained for 365 days; thereafter retain only necessary referential identity, versions/state and deduplication, removing actor/time/rationale details not needed for those links. Minimal referential rows remain while their related record exists.

## State transitions

- Guide: create draft → append draft revision → submit exact working head → publish or reject exact submitted head. Editing after submission creates a new draft and invalidates old previews. Rejection leaves any prior eligible published head intact. Publish replaces the head; retire withholds all current guide bodies and cannot be undone under that identity.
- Assignment: create current binding → withdraw, or replace atomically with a new assignment and supersede old. Source/authority/revision changes compute unavailable/obsolete immediately; no worker is needed to deny actions.
- Checkpoint: save draft revisions → submit exact head → request_changes or verify. Request changes allows a new attempt. No override of missing evidence/prerequisites. Historical decisions remain immutable; loss of evidence removes current credit.
- Review: prepare exact preview → reauthorize and compare all bindings → commit one decision/receipt, or fail with no side effects. Expired preview never authorizes a new action.

All new persistence applies only to 013. Existing domain reviews, conversation ownership, staffing and shared-publication rights remain unchanged.
